## Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 26, 2026

Shabbos!! ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Shabbos!! mobile application (the "App") uses, how it is used, and your choices.

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Process

### A. Location Data
- The App may access your device location (latitude/longitude) if you grant permission.
- Location is used only to calculate accurate local candle-lighting and related times.
- You can also choose a manual city/location instead of GPS.

### B. App Settings and Preferences (Stored on Device)
- The App stores settings locally on your device (for example: language, selected location, notification options, reminder timing, and onboarding status).
- This information is stored using on-device app storage and is not sold or shared by us.

### C. Notification and Alarm Metadata
- To deliver reminders, the App stores notification scheduling details on your device (for example: notification type and scheduled time).
- This data is used only for App functionality.

### D. No Account Data
- The App does not require account creation.
- We do not collect usernames, passwords, or profile data.

## 2. How Information Is Used

We use data only to operate the App, including:
- calculating location-based candle-lighting and holiday times;
- scheduling reminders and alarms;
- saving your preferences and app behavior choices;
- improving reliability of reminders on your device.

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App retrieves Jewish calendar and zmanim-related timing data from:
- Hebcal ([https://www.hebcal.com](https://www.hebcal.com))

To request this data, the App may send location-related query parameters (such as coordinates/timezone) to Hebcal endpoints. Use of third-party services is subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

The App may also rely on operating system services (for example, location/geocoding/notification frameworks) provided by Apple and Google.

## 4. Data Sharing and Sale

- We do not sell personal data.
- We do not share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers.
- We do not run third-party advertising SDKs in the App.

## 5. Data Retention

- Most App data is stored locally on your device and remains there until you clear app data or uninstall the App.
- Data sent to third-party services (such as Hebcal requests) is governed by those services.

## 6. Your Choices and Controls

You can:
- deny or revoke location permission in device settings;
- disable notifications in device settings or in the App;
- stop data storage by uninstalling the App or clearing app data.

If you revoke permissions, some features may not function as intended.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 8. Security

We take reasonable steps to keep data handling minimal and limited to what is needed for App functionality. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## 9. International Use

The App can be used internationally. If you use the App outside your country, data requests to third-party services may be processed in other jurisdictions under their policies.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted with a revised "Last updated" date.
